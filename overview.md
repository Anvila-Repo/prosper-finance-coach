# Overview

## Mission
Prosper exists to turn personal budgeting from an intimidating chore into an empowering act of self-care. Many individuals avoid looking at their accounts out of anxiety; Prosper creates a safe, motivating environment to confront, manage, and optimize money habits.

## Key Capabilities
- **Custom Budget Planning:** Helping users choose and build a budgeting framework that matches their lifestyle (e.g., envelope system, 50/30/20).
- **Expense Optimization:** Guiding users through expense audits to identify sneaky subscriptions and areas for mindful cuts.
- **Savings Tracking:** Crafting realistic, structured plans for emergency funds, travel goals, or big purchases.
- **Behavioral Accountability:** Serving as a gentle sounding board for impulsive spending urges and celebrating financial victories.
